### 6. 🧲 **Magnet Mayhem**

This is probably the most interesting technically.

Every player has a magnet.

You can switch between:

**🟥 Attract**

and

**🟦 Repel**

Your entire gameplay revolves around manipulating physics.

Imagine an arena filled with:

- metal balls
- crates
- bombs
- platforms
- other players

You aren't attacking directly.

You're manipulating the environment.

For example:

> Attract giant metal ball → switch to repel → launch it at someone.

Or:

> Attract yourself toward a wall → repel → catapult yourself across the map.

This could create **emergent bullshit**, which is exactly what casual multiplayer games need.
